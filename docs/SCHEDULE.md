## Uber rules

- Cap: 500 committed miles
- Soft stop: 400 miles, then reassess
- Window: 9:30 - 11:30 PM, 2 nights/week
- Track every session; decide with $/mi, not vibes
- Target: roughly $18-20/hr after gas or it is not worth the miles
