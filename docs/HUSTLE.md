# Hustle playbook

Product builder who turns messy operations into clear tools. Marketer by trade. Builder by instinct.

## App IA

Hustle is one command center: Brief + tabs **Flow | Ramp | Scripts | Log**. Progress rails show weekly flow and 4-week ramp completion.

## Niche

Orlando local service businesses: landing pages ($300-600), social/GBP retainers ($150-300/mo).

## Lead with proof

Demo Drive Hub: Next.js + Firebase, pitched across 3 Tesla stores, 30+ users. Built from floor friction.

Secondary proof when relevant: Intertek reporting (~20% less manual work), Innovations doc digitization (300+ docs), FedEx CRM outcomes.

## Warm first

Family friends, Full Sail, Intertek / Innovations / FedEx alumni. Trust transfers faster than cold pitch.

## Weekly flow (Monday 1-3pm)

1. Research 5 Orlando targets
2. Post once that you are taking 1-2 projects
3. Send 5 warm DMs
4. Follow up open threads older than 3 days

## 4-week ramp

W1 polish case study + 5 warm messages. W2 Contra/Upwork niche + 2 FB groups. W3 follow-ups. W4 first call or paid project.

Uber caps. Freelance does not.
