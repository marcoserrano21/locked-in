# Elevator pitch

Positioning line (keep this):

**The schedule OS that makes "did I actually do it?" obvious on your phone.**

No login. Streak on core only. Fitness and Today in one lane. Export is the account.

---

## 8 seconds

Most habit apps track intent. This one tracks the next block. You check it when you do it. The streak only counts core.

## 30 seconds

Locked-In is a phone-first PWA for people who already know what they should do and still need proof they did it.

You lock a 7 / 14 / 30 / 90 day track. Today shows one next block. Check it. Seal the day. Soft targets never inflate the streak.

Gym is in the same app: timer, sets, rest, Complete or End. Money has a ceiling, not a badge. Data stays on the device. Export is your backup.

## 2 minutes (founder)

I built this because I was stacking Tesla shifts, school deadlines, a mileage cap, Monday outreach, and gym days, then lying to myself with a pretty tracker.

The lie is always the same. You design for the fantasy week. Twelve habits. A dashboard. A streak you can save with a water log.

So the product is mean on purpose. Core blocks only. Time highlights now; it does not lock the check. If you edit the plan mid-lock, it gets logged for Day 30. Uber stops being cute at 400 miles.

Fitness is depth, not the whole product. You can log a session like Strong, then come back to Today and seal the day. Proof is a share card, not a BI report.

It is local-first because I did not want a login wall in front of a 30-day lock. That is a real trade. Wipe the phone, lose the track, unless you exported. We say that out loud.

I am still figuring out the Hustle tab name. Fitness-first people should not have to decode "Growth." The loop I trust is: check core, seal day, optional lift, share proof.

If you try it: install from the link. Add to Home Screen. Seal one day. That is the whole pitch.

## What we are not saying

- Not "AI-powered." Coach Brain is rules. Say that.
- Not medical advice. Fitness is a log.
- Not "replaces Strong and Notion and your bank app." It is the next block plus proof.
- Not "syncs everywhere." It does not.

## Proof points (say one, not five)

1. Isolated guest slots. Their phone, their track.
2. Core-only streak. Soft targets cannot save it.
3. Complete vs End on a workout. Partial seal is honest.
4. Miles soft-stop at 400, hard ceiling at 500.
5. Installable PWA, offline after first load.
