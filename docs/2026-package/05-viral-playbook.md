# Viral playbook

Viral here does not mean dancing. It means a proof object people will post without being asked, plus a founder loop that does not sound like a growth hack.

The product already has the objects: day-seal celebrate, Sunday streak toast, workout complete card. Make those the campaign. Do not invent a mascot.

Pitch lines: [`03-elevator-pitch.md`](03-elevator-pitch.md). Cadence: [`docs/BETA_MARKETING.md`](../BETA_MARKETING.md).

---

## The loop

1. Person does the thing (check core, finish a session).
2. App offers **one** card worth posting.
3. Caption is already in the card. They add a sentence or they do not.
4. Stranger sees proof, not a dashboard.
5. CTA is a DM for a slot, not ten public guest URLs.

If the card looks like a Settings screenshot, the loop is dead.

## Native proof objects

| Object | When | Why it travels |
|---|---|---|
| Day sealed stamp | All cores checked (`Celebrate.js`) | Identity. HabitKit / BeReal energy. |
| Sunday streak share | Week sealed | Recap without a TED talk. |
| Workout PR / complete | `WorkoutCompletion.js` | Gym people already post this. Strong/Hevy taught the behavior. |
| Miles soft-stop | Hit 400 | Honest, slightly uncomfortable. Not a badge. |
| Empty Monday Flow | No outreach | Shame that is useful. Only for Sam. Do not lead with this. |

**Card rules:** one number, one stamp, dark ink, mint accent, no mute paragraph. Pass light and high-contrast if you ever invert the card. Default share is dark. That is the brand.

## Formats that fit the voice

### X (launch + week 2)

Three tweets. Problem → demo → CTA.

1. Most trackers log the week you meant to have.
2. Short screen recording: Welcome → first Check → seal. No voiceover required.
3. `10-person beta. No login. Data stays on your phone. DM for a slot.`

Do not dump `beta-01` through `beta-10` in the thread.

### Instagram Reel / Stories (15s)

Open PWA → check block → seal. Text on screen: "Proof over vibes." End frame: install line.

Second Reel, fitness only: rest timer → set check → Complete. Text: "POV: you finally log sets without 12 taps."

### TikTok (optional)

Only if you already post there. Same POV. Do not start a TikTok account to save a PWA.

### LinkedIn (1x launch week)

Builder post. Local-first, no login, export is the account. What broke. What you fixed. Link in comment, not a carousel of fake metrics.

### Stories / dump

Gym group chat, Full Sail friends, Tesla coworkers you actually like. Personal DM template is in BETA_MARKETING. That channel will outperform the Reel. Say it anyway, then send the DMs.

## Scarcity without theater

Ten isolated slots is real scarcity. Use it.

- One person, one link.
- Spreadsheet: name → slot → segment (lift / builder / hustle).
- Public post: "DM for a slot." Private reply: the URL.
- If a slot is idle after 7 days, rotate it. Tell them. Do not ghost.

Fake waitlists for an app that is already live are cringe. You have ten links. Act like it.

## Founder content (the actual flywheel)

Learning-out-loud posts retain better than launch-day hype because they are true.

Topics that fit:

- "I buried Fitness under a tab named Hustle. Testers could not find the gym."
- "Setup asked for bodyweight every session. Strong does not do that."
- "Streak only counts core. Soft targets were a lie I almost shipped."
- Muscle map miss-hits. Show the bug. Then the fix.

Anti-topics: fake urgency, "AI-powered," screenshot of Profile, comparing to apps you have not opened this month.

## Caption bank (steal, then rewrite)

**Seal:** "Day sealed. Soft targets can wait. Core is what counts."

**Fitness:** "Logged the workout. Sealed the day. One app. No login."

**Local-first:** "No cloud yet. Export is the backup. I am not pretending otherwise."

**Beta:** "Trying a 10-person beta. Need honest mobile feedback on Today + Fitness. DM."

**Hire:** "I ship the OS I actually use on shift weeks. Case study in the repo."

No hashtags. No emoji icons. If a caption needs a fire emoji, the card is weak.

## What "going viral" would actually look like

A gym Reel of the completion screen, stitched by people who already post lifts. Not a Product Hunt badge. Not a waitlist counter.

Prepare for that by making the completion card readable at 9:16, with the streak or PR large enough to screenshot. Everything else is hoping.
