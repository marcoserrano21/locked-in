## Slide 2: The problem

Habit apps track intent.
Calendars track time.
Gym apps track sets.

Nobody tracks whether the **next block** actually happened.

*Notes:* Drop them into the friction. Tesla week, Full Sail, mileage cap, Monday outreach, gym. You were lying to yourself with a pretty tracker. That is the scene. Then name the pattern: fantasy week vs receipts.

## Slide 3: Unique mechanism

Two rules.

1. **Next block is the only CTA.**

## Slide 7: Beta proof

Ten isolated guest slots.
Their phone. Their track. No shared blob.
