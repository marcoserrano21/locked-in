# In-app voice

## Tone

Direct. Blunt. Human. No fluff. Coach energy, not LinkedIn energy.

Write like a friend who already knows your excuses and is not impressed by them.

## Rules

- Short sentences. Short paragraphs.
- Contractions speech is fine.
- No em dashes. Use hyphen, "to", or rephrase.
- No emoji icons in the UI.
- No corporate filler (leverage, synergy, circle back).
- Qualifiers only when true: "still testing this," "I think."

## Copy patterns

**Empty Monday money block**
> Monday money block is empty = no outreach = no clients.

**Streak at risk**
> Core blocks still open. Midnight does not care about your mood.

**Uber soft stop**
> 400 miles. Stop and reassess with numbers, not vibes.

**Mid-lock plan edit**
> Editing the plan mid-lock. This gets logged for Day 30.

**Day complete**
> Day sealed. Soft targets can wait. Core is what counts.
